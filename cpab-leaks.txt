! Title: CPAB own leak list
! Homepage: https://cyclone9.dev/cpab
! License: GPL-3.0-or-later
!
! Sites with a known data leak that the other data-leak sources miss, written here by hand. The
! data-leak notice shows each one as its own line, "Listed by CPAB", with a link to its source.
!
! One entry per line, five fields split by |:
!   domain | English name | Persian name | YYYY-MM | https source
! - domain: the site's host, without www. An entry covers that host and everything under it.
! - The names are plain text, up to 80 characters, with no < or >.
! - YYYY-MM: the month the leak became public, in the Gregorian calendar.
! - The source is an https page about the leak: a news report or the company's own notice. Never a
!   page that holds or links to the leaked data.
! Put a comment above each entry with the date it was added and why:
! ! 2026-11-08, added: news report on the leak
! example.ir | Example | نمونه | 2026-10 | https://news.example.com/report
!
! The build drops any line that breaks these rules and prints why.
