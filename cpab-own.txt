! :cpab-offscreen is CPAB's own mark: it moves the box out of view at full size. Other blockers
! do not understand it.
!
! 2026-11-06, owner report, kojaro.com: the top banner. PersianBlocker's kojaro rules no longer match
! it, and its images carry no ad address. The close button is what marks this box as an ad.
kojaro.com##div:has(> div > button[aria-label="بستن تبلیغ"])
!
! 2026-11-06, owner report, soft98.ir: banners served from the site's own hosts, which no list blocks.
! The 300x250 image sits in a hidden link and may be how the site checks for a blocker. EasyList
! blocks it by its name, so it stays loading whichever lists are on.
@@||img.soft98.ir/1/300x250.$image,domain=soft98.ir
@@||file3.soft98.ir/300x250.$image,domain=soft98.ir
! 2026-11-07, owner report, soft98.ir: with the banner images blocked and the boxes collapsed, the
! site showed its blocker warning. The images now load and the boxes move out of view at full size.
! The page's script forces display and clip-path back, inline with !important and on a timer, so
! nothing here touches those. Only the outermost box of each ad moves: one moved inside another
! would leave the outer one zero wide. #sbd, #tbdl and .tbdl hold the real software list and stay.
soft98.ir##.cbd1211:has(> .abdd):cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir##.abdd_ibd:cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir###sbds:has(.abddl):cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir###sbd > .cbdd:has(> .abdd):cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir##.abddm:not(.abdd_ibd > .abddm):cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir##.abdd:not(.cbd1211 > .abdd, .abddm > .abdd, #sbd > .cbdd > .abdd):cpab-offscreen
soft98.ir###sbd > hr:has(+ #sbds)
soft98.ir###sbd > hr:has(+ .cbdd > .abdd)
!
! 2026-11-08, owner report, varzesh3.ir: the site moved from varzesh3.com, and the list rules for it
! still name only the old domain. Its banners now load from biz-cdn.varzesh3.ir/banners/, ad links go
! through biz.varzesh3.ir/events/click/, and each ad sits in a box marked data-ad. Checked on the
! site's home page the same day.
||biz-cdn.varzesh3.ir/banners^
varzesh3.ir##[data-ad]
varzesh3.ir##a[href^="https://biz.varzesh3.ir/events/click/"]
